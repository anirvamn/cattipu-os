# Code of Conduct

CATTIPU OS follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer,
[@anirvamn](https://github.com/anirvamn). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions
and comments that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and anywhere someone represents the project.
